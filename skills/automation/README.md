# Automation

Skills that let an agent act for you in other apps.

## Model-invoked

- **[teams-hands-free](./teams-hands-free/SKILL.md)**: Read and send Microsoft Teams chats and files from the terminal, on a personal or work account. It asks before every send.

**Warning:** teams-hands-free uses Microsoft's private Teams endpoints and signs in as Microsoft's own Teams apps. It may break at any time and may conflict with Microsoft's terms of use. Use it at your own risk, with your own accounts only. On a work account, check your company's IT rules first.
