# Automation

Skills that let an agent act for you in other apps.

## Model-invoked

- **[teams-hands-free](./teams-hands-free/SKILL.md)**: Read and send personal Microsoft Teams chats and files from the terminal. It asks before every send.

**Warning:** teams-hands-free uses Microsoft's private consumer Teams endpoints and the Teams web app's client id. It may break at any time and may conflict with Microsoft's terms of use. Use it at your own risk, with your own personal account only.
